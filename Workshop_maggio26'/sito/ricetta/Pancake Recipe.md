
[Farina 00](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-la-Farina-00/) 125 g

[Whole milk](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-Latte-intero/) 200 g

[Butter](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-il-Burro/) 25 g

[Sugar](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-lo-Zucchero/) 15 g

[Eggs](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-le-Uova-di-gallina/) medium 2

[Baking powder](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-Lievito-in-polvere-per-dolci/) 6 g

[Fine salt](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/ricette-con-Sale-fino/) 1 pinch

Butter as needed to grease the pan

Pancake recipe: first, melt the butter in a small saucepan and let it cool slightly. Separate the egg yolks from the whites. Pour the melted butter and milk into the bowl with the yolks. Mix with a whisk, then add the sifted flour.

Sift the baking powder too and incorporate the dry ingredients with the whisk, then add the salt and mix again until smooth. Now take the bowl with the egg whites and start whipping them with electric beaters.

Gradually add the sugar and continue beating until stiff peaks form. Fold the whipped egg whites into the yolk mixture.

Gently mix with the whisk from bottom to top to incorporate evenly. You're ready to cook the pancakes: heat a non-stick pan with a little butter using a paper towel, then pour a ladleful of batter into the center.

Cook over medium heat for a couple of minutes; when small bubbles appear on the surface, gently flip the pancake with a spatula and cook briefly on the other side until golden. As they're done, transfer them to a plate without stacking, so they don't deflate.
